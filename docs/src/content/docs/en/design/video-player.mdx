Both portals play lesson videos with the same player, with its own control bar in place of the browser's.

## Resume

The player saves the student's position about every 10 seconds while playing, and when they pause, seek, or leave the page. Next time they open the video it jumps to that point, stays paused, and shows "Resumed at m:ss" with a **Start over** button. The instructor's preview does not save a position.

## Speed, volume and preferences

Speed runs from 0.5x to 2x (0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.25, 1.5, 1.75, 2). Speed, volume, mute and the captions choice are remembered in the browser; if storage is unavailable, the defaults apply silently.

## Keyboard shortcuts

They work while the player has focus, and are ignored in text fields and with Ctrl, Alt or Meta held.

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| Space, K | Play or pause |
| J, L | Back, forward 10 seconds |
| Left, Right | Back, forward 5 seconds |
| Up, Down | Volume up, down 5% |
| M | Mute |
| C | Captions on or off |
| F | Fullscreen |
| `<`, `>` | Previous, next speed |
| 0 to 9 | Jump to 0% to 90% of the video |

## Captions

When a video has caption tracks, the player shows a captions menu: **Off**, then each track by language, with the default marked. A track that fails to load is skipped with a short notice and the video keeps playing.

- **Auto-generated:** when a video finishes transcoding, Amazon Transcribe generates a track in the background. This never delays the video becoming ready.
- **Uploaded by an instructor:** an instructor can attach a WebVTT (`.vtt`) file to a ready video. Uploading for a language that already has a track replaces it and keeps its default status, so an upload that replaces the auto-generated track becomes the default. A later Transcribe result never overwrites an uploaded track.
- **Limits:** a file can be at most 1 MB, a video has at most 10 tracks, and there is one track per language.

### Caption status

A video's `captionsStatus` is `pending` while Transcribe runs, then `ready` or `failed`. A job still pending after 2 hours is reported as `failed`. A failure only affects captions; the video itself stays playable. The instructor's lesson row shows the status and the track count.

## Transcript

A **Transcript** button in the control bar opens a panel with every line of the video's captions, each with its time. It is only in the student lesson player, and only when the video has at least one caption track. The preview and the instructor portal don't show it. On wide screens the panel sits beside the player at the player's height; on narrow ones it sits below, at most 20rem or half the viewport high. Nothing scrolls sideways at phone width.

- **Which track:** the one the captions menu has chosen. Choosing another language changes the lines and the panel's name, "Transcript (language)". With captions **Off**, the panel still shows the remembered track, or the default. If no track's file loads, the panel says "This transcript couldn't be loaded." and the video keeps playing.
- **Choosing a line:** clicking or tapping a line seeks to its start and plays.
- **Following:** the current line is marked (a left border and a heavier weight, plus `aria-current="true"`) and the panel scrolls itself to keep it in view. It never scrolls the page and never moves focus. Scrolling, wheeling, touching or moving focus inside the panel pauses following and shows **Jump to current line**; that button, or choosing a line, resumes it. With reduced motion set, the scroll isn't animated.
- **Screen readers:** there is no live region, because the captions and the audio already carry the live content. Each line reads as its time in words ("12 minutes 40 seconds") and then its text.
- **Remembered open state:** open or closed is remembered in the browser with the other player preferences (`transcriptOpen`, closed by default).

### Transcript keys

The panel is outside the player's shortcut container, so the shortcuts above don't apply inside it: Space, arrows and the letter keys never change the video from there.

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| Tab | Into the list, onto the current line (the first line before any cue); one more Tab leaves it |
| Down, Up | Focus the next, previous line |
| Home, End | Focus the first, last line |
| Enter, Space | Seek to the focused line and play; focus stays on it |

### Built in the browser

There is no transcript API. The player already fetches each track's WebVTT file for the captions overlay; the panel parses the same text once (`parseWebVtt`, in `@discava/common-web-utils/webvtt`) into cues, so it makes no extra request and has exactly the access the captions have. Cue text is reduced to plain text (cue settings, `<v>`, `<c>`, `<i>` and timestamp tags dropped, the five WebVTT entities decoded) and rendered as text nodes. The whole transcript is rendered, with no virtual list, so find-in-page and a screen reader's reading commands work on it. The indexer for <Link path="design/search">transcript search</Link> uses the same parser, so the panel and the index agree on what a line is.

### Opening a lesson at a moment

A lesson's address takes two optional parameters: `?item=<contentItemId>&t=<seconds>`. With `item` naming a video in the lesson, the lesson scrolls to it, moves focus to its player, seeks to `t`, stays paused, and shows "Starting at m:ss" with no **Start over** button. `t` takes the place of the saved position for that visit, and the seek saves nothing. Anything else in the parameters is ignored and the lesson opens as usual. The parameters grant nothing: the lesson still loads through the usual enrolment and visibility checks. A transcript search result links this way.

## Questions

An instructor can put questions on a video (see <Link path="design/quizzes">Quizzes</Link> for how they are stored and saved). A video without questions plays exactly as above and makes no question request.

- **When a question is asked:** when normal playback reaches the moment of a question the learner hasn't answered or skipped on this visit, the video pauses and the question appears over it inside the player, so fullscreen is kept. Keyboard focus moves to the question and its heading is announced. Several questions at one moment are asked one after another, in the instructor's order. A question at 0:00 is asked when playback starts.
- **Answering:** the learner chooses an option and presses **Submit answer**, then sees whether it was right (an icon and words, not colour alone), the correct options and the explanation when there is one, announced when it arrives. **Continue** resumes the video from where it stopped, playing again if it was playing. **Skip** (or Escape) closes the question, records nothing and doesn't ask it again on this visit. A multiple choice question says "Select all that apply" and is graded all-or-nothing. If saving fails, the question stays open with the choice kept and **Skip** still works.
- **Shortcuts while a question is open:** the control bar is inert and the player shortcuts (Space, arrows, number keys and the rest) don't act on the video underneath. In picture-in-picture the video returns to the page so the question can be seen.
- **Seeking:** seeking never opens a question, by dragging, a shortcut or resuming at a saved position, including on a first viewing. A question is asked only when playback reaches its moment, or when the learner chooses its marker.
- **Markers:** the seek bar has one marker per timed question and one at the end when there is an end check. Each is a button that names its time and state ("Question at 1:30, not answered"), and answered and unanswered differ by a tick and a dot as well as colour. Choosing a marker moves to that moment, pauses and opens the question: an unanswered one to answer, an answered one to review with the recorded answer, the key and the explanation. **Try again** there is practice, graded in the browser and not recorded.
- **The end check:** when the video ends and has unanswered end questions, the first appears by itself ("Question 1 of 2"), then the next, and a summary says how many end questions the learner has answered correctly. Each can be skipped and the check can be closed. Once every end question is answered it doesn't start by itself again, and the end marker opens the answers. A question timed after the end of the video that is playing is asked with the end check.
- **Resuming before an open question:** a learner who leaves with a question open comes back just before it, so it is asked again. A learner whose saved position is after an unanswered question isn't asked it on resume; its marker shows it unanswered.
- **Recorded, and not:** the first answer to each question is recorded, and nothing else. Skips and **Try again** are not. An answer never changes whether the video is complete (that is still **Mark as done**), progress or certificates.
- **Failures:** if the questions fail to load, the video plays as if it had none. In an archived course videos play without their questions.
- **Small screens and motion:** a question taller than the video extends below it and scrolls with the page, with no sideways scroll at 375 px. With reduced motion set, it appears without animation.
