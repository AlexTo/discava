**Dashboard** in the instructor portal shows how your courses are doing. Only the instructors of a course can see its figures.

## The Dashboard

There is one card for each course you teach, whatever its status.

| Figure | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Enrolments** | Everyone who has enrolled |
| **Active this week** | Learners who did something in the course this calendar week, Monday to Sunday in UTC |
| **Completion rate** | Of the learners still enrolled, the share who completed the course, as a whole percent rounded down. "No data" until someone is enrolled |

Each card also says when its figures last changed, or "No activity yet". Choose a course's title to open its analytics page.

## A course's analytics page

### Totals

| Figure | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Enrolments** | Everyone who has enrolled |
| **Active** | Enrolled, and neither completed nor left |
| **Completed** | Completed the course |
| **Dropped** | Left the course, or had their enrolment ended |
| **Completion rate** | As on the Dashboard |
| **Active this week** | As on the Dashboard |

### 30 or 90 days

The two charts cover the last 90 days. Choose **30 days** or **90 days** at the top of the page to switch.

- **New enrolments per day**
- **Active learners per day**

Days are UTC days. Until someone enrols the page says "No learners have enrolled yet".

### Lesson drop-off

For each lesson shown to learners: of the learners who started it, the share who completed it. The lesson with the lowest share is marked **Lowest completion**. A lesson nobody has started says so.

Learners who left the course aren't counted.

### Activation

How many enrolled learners have started, as a number and a percentage. Started means they completed an item or submitted a quiz attempt. Recent learners may still start, so these are figures so far.

The card also says how many learners answered the first-lesson prompt and how many dismissed it. See <Link path="guides/lesson-discussions">Lesson discussions</Link>.

### Quizzes

There is a panel for each quiz. **Show analysis** opens:

- **Most-missed questions**: each question's attempts, misses and miss rate, the most missed first. A question with fewer than 5 attempts isn't ranked and shows "Not enough attempts".
- **Best score per student**: a chart of how the best scores are spread.

### Video questions

The **Video questions** panel lists the videos that ask questions. **Show results** on a video opens each question's moment in the video, its answers, misses and miss rate. A question with too few answers shows "Not enough answers yet".

See <Link path="guides/video-lessons">Video lessons</Link> for adding questions to a video.

## Reading the figures

- A learner counts once in a day or a week, however much they did.
- If a course's page says "Course not found", the course doesn't exist or you don't teach it.
