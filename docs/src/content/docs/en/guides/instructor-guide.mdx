This page is the overview of the instructor portal. To use the portal your account must be an instructor: if it says you aren't one, ask an administrator.

## Finding your way around

The sidebar has four pages:

| Page | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Your course count, **Waiting for grading** (assignment submissions to grade) and your latest courses |
| **Dashboard** | Enrolments, activity and completion for each course |
| **My Courses** | Every course you teach. **Open course** opens its page, where you build and manage it |
| **Earnings** | What your courses earned and what has been paid out |

The account menu has **Public profile**, **Notification settings** and **Sign out**.

A course's page is one long page. The links at the top jump to its sections: **Content**, **Landing page**, **Catalogue**, **Captions**, **Certificates**, **First-lesson prompt**, **Price** and **Announcements**. Beside the course title, **Reviews**, **Questions** and **Coupons** open pages of their own.

## Building a course

1. Choose **Create a course**, on Home or My Courses. Give it a **Course title** and a **Course summary**. The course starts as a draft.
2. In **Course content**, add a module with **New module**.
3. In the module, add lessons with **New lesson**, or an assignment with **New assignment**.
4. In a lesson, add its content: **Video**, **Text**, **Quiz** or **File**.
5. Drag modules, lessons and content by the grip to reorder them.
6. In **Catalogue details**, set the category, level, language, duration and up to 8 tags. Students filter the catalogue and search by these.
7. Add a course image.

Captions are added to every video automatically. <Link path="guides/building-a-course">Building a course</Link> walks through each step.

What else you can add:

- **Quizzes**: single choice and multiple choice questions, a pass mark, a number of attempts or unlimited, and when the correct answers are shown. **Preview as student** tries the quiz without saving anything. See <Link path="guides/quizzes-and-assignments">Quizzes and assignments</Link>.
- **Assignments**: students upload a file and you grade it, or a quiz graded automatically. An upload assignment has instructions, maximum points and an optional due date. Its **Submissions** link lists the work, and Home lists what is **Waiting for grading**. You **Return** work with points and feedback, or choose **Request resubmission**. See <Link path="guides/quizzes-and-assignments">Quizzes and assignments</Link>.
- **Questions in a video**: the **Questions** dialog on a video adds up to 20 questions, each at a time in the video or at the end. Answers never count towards progress or certificates. See <Link path="guides/video-lessons">Video lessons</Link>.
- **Certificates**: in the **Certificates** section, switch on **Issue a certificate on completion**, and optionally require passing every quiz or a minimum grade on assignments. The same section lists the certificates issued, with **Revoke** and **Restore**. See <Link path="guides/certificates">Certificates</Link>.

## Publishing and changing a course

**Publish** on the course's page makes a draft course visible to students. After that:

- New modules, lessons and content stay hidden until you choose **Publish** on each. **Hide** takes one back out of students' view.
- Removing a module, lesson or content item archives it instead of deleting it. The **Archived** section lists them, with **Restore** and **Delete permanently**. Something students have used can't be deleted permanently.
- An archived course can't be edited until you choose **Restore**.

<Link path="guides/building-a-course">Building a course</Link> has the details, and <Link path="design/live-course-editing">Live course editing</Link> describes what students see when you change a published course.

## Your landing page and profile

A published course's page, `/learn/<courseId>`, is public: visitors who aren't signed in see it, and search engines and link previews can read it.

**On the course's page**, the **Landing page** section has:

- **What students will learn** and **Requirements**: up to 10 of each, up to 160 characters each. You can reorder them with the "Move up" and "Move down" buttons. Choose **Save landing page** when you are done.
- A **preview video**: one short video, at most 5 minutes (1 to 2 minutes works best), uploaded as its own file, not one of your lessons. Captions are added automatically. **Replace** keeps the old one playing until the new one is ready, and **Remove video** goes back to the course image. Anyone can watch it, but no lesson video becomes public.

The **Landing page checklist** above it shows what is missing: learning outcomes, requirements, a preview video, a course image and your profile's headline and photo. It never stops you publishing. **View public page** opens the page once the course is published.

**Your public profile** (**Public profile** in the account menu) has:

| Field | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Display name | Up to 60 characters |
| Handle | 3 to 30 characters. Your address is `/instructors/<handle>` |
| Headline | Up to 100 characters |
| Bio | Up to 2,000 characters |
| Photo | Added after the profile is first saved |

The profile is created with your first course, and it is public while you teach at least one published course. Every instructor of a published course is shown on its page, and you can't opt out.

Changes reach search engines and link previews within 5 minutes. An archived course, or a profile with no published course left, stops being found at the same pace. See <Link path="design/public-pages">Public pages</Link> for how it works.

## Caption languages

Captions are added to every video automatically. On the course's page, the **Caption languages** section lets you choose other languages to have them translated into. Videos you already have and videos you add later both follow your choice, previews included. Translating a video takes a few minutes after its captions are ready.

The section is missing when captions are switched off for the site, and it says so when caption translation isn't available.

- **Your own captions always win.** If you upload captions for a language, that upload is kept and never replaced by a translation. To correct a translation, upload your own captions for that language in the video's Captions dialog.
- **Replacing the video's main captions** removes its translated tracks, and they are translated again from the new captions.
- **Removing a language** removes its translated tracks from every video. Captions you uploaded stay.
- **The Captions dialog** shows each language's state: translating, translated, uploaded, failed, too long, no room (a video has at most 10 tracks) or not translated, with the reason. Automatic captions that aren't in your course's language aren't translated: upload captions in the video's language instead.
- **Translate again** in the dialog asks for one video's missing translations once more. **Translate missing captions again** in the section does it for the whole course.
- **Machine translation can be wrong.** Students see translated tracks marked "(auto-translated)". Each caption line is translated on its own, so check the languages you can read before relying on them.

## Free preview lessons

A free preview lets anyone watch a lesson before they enrol, which is the quickest way to show what your course is like.

1. In **Course content**, choose **Make free preview** on a lesson.
2. Confirm with **Make free preview**.

The lesson shows a "Free preview" badge, and its title links to the preview from your landing page. The badge reads "not public yet" until the lesson, its module and the course are published.

- Visitors see the lesson's videos, text and files without signing in or paying. Nothing is saved for them: no progress, no video position, no discussion.
- **Quizzes stay locked.** A visitor sees the quiz's title and number of questions, but not the questions, and has to enrol to take it.
- **Assignment lessons can't be marked.** Only ordinary lessons can.
- To stop it, choose **Remove free preview**. New access stops at once, and the preview address then says the preview is no longer available. A video someone is already playing can run for up to an hour, and a file link for a few minutes. Hiding or archiving the lesson, its module or the course has the same effect.

## Charging for a course

A course is free until you give it a price in its **Price** section. An administrator must connect Stripe first.

- <Link path="guides/payments">Paid courses and payments</Link> covers pricing, discount codes (**Coupons**) and what students see.
- <Link path="guides/earnings-and-payouts">Earnings and payouts</Link> covers your share of each sale and how you are paid.

## Talking to students

- <Link path="guides/announcements">Announcements</Link>: post a message to everyone enrolled, from the course's **Announcements** section.
- <Link path="guides/lesson-discussions">Lesson discussions</Link>: answer students' questions and moderate posts, from the course's **Questions** page. The **First-lesson prompt** section sets the question new students are asked in the first lesson.
- <Link path="guides/reviews">Ratings and reviews</Link>: read reviews and respond to them, from the course's **Reviews** page.
- <Link path="guides/notifications">Notifications</Link>: what you and your students are told, in the bell and by email.

## Seeing how a course is doing

**Dashboard** shows each course's enrolments, learners active this week and completion rate. Open a course to see the last 30 or 90 days: new enrolments and active learners per day, where learners stop (lesson drop-off), how many started after enrolling, and results for each quiz and for questions in videos. See <Link path="guides/course-analytics">Course analytics</Link>.
