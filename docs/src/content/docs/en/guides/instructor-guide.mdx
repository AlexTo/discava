## Your landing page and profile

A published course's page, `/learn/<courseId>`, is public: visitors who aren't signed in see it, and search engines and link previews can read it.

**On the course** (My Courses, then the course's settings), the **Landing page** card has:

- **Outcomes** ("What you'll learn") and **requirements**: up to 10 of each, up to 160 characters each. You can reorder them with the "Move up" and "Move down" buttons.
- A **preview video**: one short video, at most 5 minutes (1 to 2 minutes works best), uploaded as its own file, not one of your lessons. Captions are added automatically. Replacing it keeps the old one playing until the new one is ready, and Remove goes back to the course image. Anyone can watch it, but no lesson video becomes public.

A **Landing page checklist** shows what is missing: outcomes, requirements, a preview, a course image and your profile. It never stops you publishing. **View public page** opens the page once the course is published.

**Your public profile** (Public profile in the account menu) has your display name, a handle (your address is `/instructors/<handle>`), a headline, a bio and a photo. It is created with your first course, and it is public while you teach at least one published course. Every instructor of a published course is shown on its page, and you can't opt out.

Changes reach search engines and link previews within 5 minutes. An archived course, or a profile with no published course left, stops being found at the same pace. See <Link path="design/public-pages">Public pages</Link> for how it works.

## Caption languages

Captions are added to every video automatically. On the course's settings, the **Caption languages** card lets you choose other languages to have them translated into. Videos you already have and videos you add later both follow your choice, previews included. Translating a video takes a few minutes after its captions are ready.

- **Your own captions always win.** If you upload captions for a language, that upload is kept and never replaced by a translation. To correct a translation, upload your own captions for that language in the video's Captions dialog.
- **Replacing the video's main captions** removes its translated tracks, and they are translated again from the new captions.
- **Removing a language** removes its translated tracks from every video. Captions you uploaded stay.
- **The Captions dialog** shows each language's state: translating, translated, uploaded, failed, too long, no room (a video has at most 10 tracks) or not translated, with the reason. Automatic captions that aren't in your course's language aren't translated: upload captions in the video's language instead. **Translate again** asks for the missing translations once more, for one video; **Translate missing captions again** on the card does it for the whole course.
- **Machine translation can be wrong.** Students see translated tracks marked "(auto-translated)". Each caption line is translated on its own, so check the languages you can read before relying on them.

## Free preview lessons

A free preview lets anyone watch a lesson before they enrol, which is the quickest way to show what your course is like. In the course's curriculum, choose **Make free preview** on a lesson and confirm. The lesson shows a "Free preview" badge, and its title links to the preview from your landing page. The badge reads "not public yet" until the course is published.

- Visitors see the lesson's videos, text and files without signing in or paying. Nothing is saved for them: no progress, no video position, no discussion.
- **Quizzes stay locked.** A visitor sees the quiz's title and number of questions, but not the questions, and has to enrol to take it.
- **Assignment lessons can't be marked.** Only ordinary lessons can.
- To stop it, choose **Remove free preview**. New access stops at once, and the preview address then says the preview is no longer available. A video someone is already playing can run for up to an hour, and a file link for a few minutes. Hiding or archiving the lesson, its module or the course has the same effect.
