When a student buys your course, your share is recorded within seconds and paid to you through Stripe.

## How earnings are calculated

For each sale:

> net = amount paid − platform fee

- The **amount paid** is what the student paid, after any coupon. A **discount** lowers it, and so lowers your share: you earn on the amount paid, never on the list price.
- The **platform fee** is a percentage of the amount paid, set by the platform (20% unless it was changed). A sale keeps the fee it was recorded with.
- If a course has **several instructors**, they share each sale equally.
- A **refund** takes back your share of the refunded amount, and returns the fee on it. A full refund brings the sale to zero.

Each currency is kept apart. Nothing is converted.

A sale counts on the day it was paid, and a refund on the day it was made, so a refund of last month's sale lowers this month's figures. Days are UTC days.

## The Earnings page

**Earnings** in the instructor portal's sidebar has four parts.

**Payout account**: its status and the one thing to do about it. See [Setting up payouts](#setting-up-payouts).

**Balance**, per currency:

| Figure | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **On hold** | Earned, but still inside the hold period. The page shows the next release date |
| **Waiting for account setup** | Past the hold period, but you have no active payout account |
| **Ready** | Will go out with the next payout |

**Report**: the current month to start with. Pick another month, or choose **Custom range** and set a From and To date up to 366 days apart.

- For each course it shows the number of orders, gross, discounts, refunds, platform fee and net, with a total per currency.
- **Show orders** on a course lists each sale and refund with an order reference. It never shows who bought.
- Days are counted in UTC.

**Payouts**: each payout's date, amount and status.

## Setting up payouts

1. Open **Earnings**.
2. On the **Payout account** card, choose **Set up payouts**.
3. Give Stripe your identity and bank details on its own pages. Stripe sends you back to Earnings when you finish.

The card shows where you are:

| Status | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Not set up** | **Set up payouts** |
| **In progress** | **Continue setup**: Stripe's form isn't finished |
| **Active** | Nothing. **Open Stripe dashboard** takes you to your Stripe account |
| **Restricted** | **Fix account**: Stripe needs something more from you |

If the card says "Payouts aren't available yet", the platform hasn't connected Stripe. Ask an administrator.

Earnings are recorded whether or not you have set up payouts. They wait until you do.

## When you are paid

- Each sale is **held** for a number of days set by the platform (14 on the production stage unless it was changed), so a refund in that time never has to be taken back from you.
- A payout run happens **once a day**. It sends everything that is ready, one payout per currency.
- A balance below the **minimum payout** (10.00 unless it was changed) waits for a later run.

A payout's status is one of:

- **Pending**: it is being sent.
- **Sent**: Stripe has accepted it.
- **Failed**: Stripe refused it, with the reason. Its earnings go back into your balance and are tried again.

## Refunds after you were paid

A refund made after its sale was paid out leaves a **negative balance**. It is deducted from your next payout. Nothing is taken from your bank account.

## Held earnings

If the platform changes the Stripe account it takes payments with, earnings from before the change that hadn't been paid show as **Held**: "Earned before the platform changed its payment account. Contact the platform to be paid." Your payout account shows as not set up, because it belonged to the old account. Set it up again for sales from now on.

## For administrators

- Payouts need **Connect** enabled on the platform's Stripe account. Without it, an instructor who chooses **Set up payouts** is told "This platform can't set up payout accounts yet".
- A restricted key also needs to manage connected accounts, account links, login links and transfers, in addition to what <Link path="guides/payments">taking payments</Link> needs.
- The fee, the hold period and the minimum payout are set per stage. The development stage holds sales for 1 day, not 14. See <Link path="deploy/configuration">Stage configuration</Link>.
- Switching Stripe account or mode holds every instructor's unpaid earnings. Let the daily run pay what is ready first, and pay held earnings by hand.

## Limitations

- No tax forms, invoices or tax calculation.
- No currency conversion.
- Disputes and chargebacks don't appear in the report.
