You can search, browse and read course pages without an account. You sign in to enrol.

## Search

The search box in the header reads "What do you want to learn?".

1. Type at least 3 characters. Matching courses are suggested as you type.
2. Pick a suggestion with the arrow keys and Enter, or by choosing it, to open that course.
3. Or press Enter without picking one to see every result.

On the results page you can:

- **Sort by** Relevance, Highest rated or Newest.
- Filter by category, level, language, duration and tags.
- Set a **Minimum rating**: 4.5, 4.0, 3.5 or 3.0 and up.
- Choose **Clear filters** to start again.

<Link path="guides/reviews">Ratings and reviews</Link> explains how ratings affect the order.

## Browse

- **Browse courses**, at `/courses`, lists the catalogue. Choose a category to narrow it, or **All**.
- A category has its own page, with a search box for that category.
- A category the platform has archived says it is no longer available, with a link to **Browse all courses**.

## The course page

A published course's page is public. It shows what the course covers, its content, its price or "Free", its rating and reviews, and its instructors.

- **Play preview** plays the course's preview video, if it has one.
- A lesson marked **Free preview** opens without signing in or enrolling. You can watch its videos, read its text and download its files. Its quizzes stay locked: "Enrol to take this quiz". Nothing is saved for you until you enrol.
- **View profile** on an instructor's card opens their profile, with the courses they teach.

If an instructor stops offering a preview, its address says "This preview is no longer available." with **View the course**.

## Enrolling in a free course

1. On the course page, choose **Enrol now**.
2. If you aren't signed in, choose **Continue to sign in** and sign in. You come back to the course.
3. The first lesson you haven't completed opens, with "You're enrolled in" and the course name.

If you enrol from a free preview lesson, you stay on that lesson and can choose **Start this lesson**.

A course with a price shows **Buy now** instead. See <Link path="guides/payments">Paid courses and payments</Link>.

## Leaving a course

For a course you didn't pay for, the course page shows **Leave course**.

1. Choose **Leave course**.
2. Confirm with **Leave course**.

Your enrolment ends and your progress is kept. You can enrol again at any time and carry on, unless the course has been given a price since: then you would need to buy it.

A course you paid for has no **Leave course**. Ask for a refund instead.

## My Learning

**My Learning** in the header lists your courses in two tabs, **In progress** and **Completed**. Each card shows your progress and one button:

| Button | When |
| --- | --- |
| **Start** | You haven't completed anything yet |
| **Continue** | You are part of the way through |
| **Review** | You completed the course |
| **See what's new** | You completed the course and the instructor has added to it since |

When you are signed in, the home page welcomes you back with **Continue learning** for the course you were on, your other courses, and courses to start.

The course page of a course you are enrolled in shows your progress as a percentage, with **Start learning** or **Continue learning**. A completed course shows **Completed**, the date, and **Review course**.
