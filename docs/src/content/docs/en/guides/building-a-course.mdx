A course is made of modules. A module holds lessons, and a lesson holds its content: videos, text, quizzes and files. You build all of it on the course's page in the instructor portal.

## Create the course

1. Open **My Courses** in the instructor portal.
2. Choose **Create a course**.
3. Enter a **Course title** (up to 200 characters) and a **Course summary**.
4. Create the course. It starts as a **Draft**, which students can't see.

**Open course** on a course's card opens its page.

## Add the content

Work in the **Course content** section of the course's page.

1. Choose **New module** and name it.
2. In the module, choose **New lesson**. For graded work, choose **New assignment** instead.
3. In the lesson, add its content: **Video**, **Text**, **Quiz** or **File**.
4. Drag a module, lesson or content item by its grip to move it.

Captions are added to every video automatically.

- <Link path="guides/quizzes-and-assignments">Quizzes and assignments</Link> covers quiz settings, assignments and grading.
- <Link path="guides/video-lessons">Video lessons</Link> covers captions and questions inside a video.

## Describe it for the catalogue

In the **Catalogue details** section, set:

| Field | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| Category | One of the platform's categories, or "No category" |
| Level | Beginner, Intermediate or Advanced |
| Language | The language the course is taught in |
| Duration | Hours, up to 500 |
| Tags | Up to 8, up to 30 characters each |

Students filter the catalogue and search by these. Add a course image as well: it shows on the course's card and page.

The **Landing page** section, your public profile and free preview lessons are covered in the <Link path="guides/instructor-guide">Instructor guide</Link>.

## Publish

Choose **Publish** at the top of the course's page and confirm. Students can now see the course and everything in it.

Two things change once a course is published:

- **New content starts hidden.** A module, lesson or content item you add shows a **Hidden** badge until you choose **Publish** on it. **Hide** takes something back out of students' view.
- **Removing archives.** Removing a module, lesson or content item archives it instead of deleting it.

See <Link path="design/live-course-editing">Live course editing</Link> for what students see when you change a published course.

## Archived content

The **Archived** section, at the bottom of the course's page, lists what you removed from a published course.

- **Restore** puts an entry back at the end of where it was.
- **Delete permanently** removes it for good. It is refused for anything students have used: "Students have used it, so it stays archived."

## An archived course

An archived course can't be edited. Its page says "This course is archived, so it can't be edited. Restore it to make changes." Choose **Restore** at the top of the page to work on it again.

The instructor portal has no button to archive a whole course.
