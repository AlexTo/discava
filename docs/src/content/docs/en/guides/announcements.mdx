An announcement is a short message from an instructor to the students enrolled in a course. It has a title (up to 120 characters) and formatted text.

## For instructors

Open the course from **My Courses** and go to its **Announcements** section, near the bottom of the page.

- **Post**: enter a title and the text, then choose **Post announcement**. Only a published course takes new announcements. A draft course shows "Publish the course to post announcements."
- **Edit**: choose **Edit** on an announcement, change the title or text, then **Save changes**. Students see the new text with an "edited" mark.
- **Delete**: choose **Delete** and confirm. The announcement disappears from the course home. This can't be undone.

What students are told:

- Posting notifies each enrolled student once, in the bell and, unless they switched it off, by email.
- Editing sends nothing.
- Deleting doesn't take back the notification students already received.
- Posting the same announcement twice, for example by double clicking, still posts it once.

In an archived course you can still edit and delete announcements, but not post new ones.

## Who can read them

- Students enrolled in the course read announcements in the course home, newest first. This still works after the course is archived.
- A student who left the course, a student of another course and visitors can't read them.
- The instructors teaching the course read them in the instructor portal.

## For developers

Announcements add no delivery of their own. Posting writes the announcement and a request to notify the course's students in one transaction, through the notifications producer contract (`notifyCourseStudents`, the type `announcement.posted`). The notification fan-out then delivers it. See <Link path="guides/notifications">Notifications</Link> for how that works.
