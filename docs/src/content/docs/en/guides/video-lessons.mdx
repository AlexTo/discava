## The player

The player's controls set the playback speed, the captions, the volume and full screen, and open the transcript. Speeds run from 0.5 to 2 in steps of 0.25. The player remembers your speed, volume and caption choices for the next video.

Keyboard shortcuts work while the player has focus and you aren't typing in a field:

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Space or K | Play or pause |
| J | Back 10 seconds |
| L | Forward 10 seconds |
| Left arrow | Back 5 seconds |
| Right arrow | Forward 5 seconds |
| Up arrow, Down arrow | Volume up, volume down |
| M | Mute or unmute |
| C | Captions on or off |
| F | Full screen |
| `<` and `>` | Slower, faster |
| 0 to 9 | Jump to that tenth of the video: 5 is halfway |

## Captions and the transcript

Captions are added to every video automatically. The captions menu lists the languages the video has, and **Off**.

- A language marked "(auto-translated)" is a machine translation. It can be wrong.
- **Transcript** opens the text of the video. **Jump to current line** brings it back to where the video is.

Instructors choose which languages captions are translated into, and can upload their own captions. See the <Link path="guides/instructor-guide">Instructor guide</Link>.

## Searching what was said

You can search the captions of every video in a course you are enrolled in.

1. In the course player, find **Search transcripts**.
2. Type at least 2 characters in "Search what was said".
3. Choose **Search**.
4. Choose a result to open that lesson at that moment.

The page lists the moments that match, up to the 20 best. If there are more, add a word to narrow the search. If nothing matches, check the spelling, or try the words in the captions' language.

## Questions in a video

An instructor can make a video stop and ask a question, at a moment in the video or at the end.

### For students

1. When the video stops, read the question. A multiple choice question says "Select all that apply".
2. Pick your answer and choose **Submit answer**, or choose **Skip**.
3. Read whether you were right ("Correct" or "Not quite"), the correct answer and the **Explanation**, if there is one.
4. Choose **Continue** to carry on watching.

- One answer per question is recorded. A multiple choice question is right only when every choice is right.
- **Try again** is practice: "Practice: this answer isn't recorded".
- Answers never count towards your progress, the course's quizzes or a certificate.
- An archived course asks no questions.
- If the instructor changed a question since you last saw it, it says "This question was updated. Check it before answering."

### For instructors

1. In **Course content**, open the **Questions** dialog on a video.
2. Play or drag the video to the moment you want, and choose **Add a question here**. For a question after the video, choose **Add an end question**.
3. Write the question, its options and which are correct.
4. Adjust **Ask at** if needed: a time such as 1:30, or **At the end**.
5. Save.

- A video takes up to 20 questions.
- **Preview as student** plays the video with its questions. Answers are checked and not recorded. **Stop previewing** goes back to editing.
- You can change the questions after students have answered. Answers already recorded are kept and aren't regraded.

The results show on the course's analytics page, under **Video questions**. See <Link path="guides/course-analytics">Course analytics</Link>.
