Every lesson has a **Questions** section where the students enrolled in the course ask questions and answer each other, and where the course's instructors step in.

## For students

In a lesson, under the content, the **Questions** section lists the lesson's questions, newest first.

- **Ask** with a title (up to 120 characters) and details.
- **Reply** to a question from its thread. A thread holds up to 500 replies.
- **Upvote** a question or reply written by someone else. Press the button again to take the upvote back.
- **Accept** an answer: the person who asked can **Mark as answer** on any reply. It shows pinned above the other replies as **Accepted answer**, and the question counts as answered. **Remove answer** takes it back.
- **Report** a post by someone else that breaks the rules. Instructors see it in their Reported list.
- **Delete** your own question or reply. Deleting your question also deletes its accepted answer.

### The first-lesson prompt

When you enrol, you land in the course's first lesson, and its **Questions** section shows a prompt above the list, for example "What do you want to be able to do with what this course teaches?". It has one text box, **Post** and **Not now**.

- You can answer once. **Post** adds your answer to the lesson's list, marked **Answer to the course prompt**. Classmates can reply to it and upvote it like a question.
- **Not now** closes the prompt without posting anything.
- Either way the prompt doesn't come back, on any device, even if you delete your answer.
- Everyone in the course can read your answer, and so can its instructors.

It appears only in the first lesson shown to students, and doesn't take keyboard focus when the lesson opens.

You are told through the bell when someone replies to your question and when your reply is accepted. See the notifications guide.

## For instructors

The course's **Questions** page in the instructor portal has three views:

- **Unanswered**: questions with no instructor reply and no accepted answer, oldest first. Replying as an instructor (shown with an **Instructor** badge) takes the question off the list. You don't need to be enrolled.
- **Reported**: posts students reported, with the number of reports. **Hide** a post, **Delete** it or **Dismiss reports**.
- **By lesson**: every thread in a chosen lesson.

Instructors can also mark an answer in any thread.

### The first-lesson prompt

In the course's settings you can replace the prompt's text (plain text, 1 to 200 characters). Clearing it brings back the default. Changing it doesn't show the prompt again to learners who already answered or dismissed it.

Answers to the prompt are not in **Unanswered**, because they aren't questions waiting for an instructor. Read them under **By lesson**, in the course's first lesson. You can reply to, hide or delete them like any post, and a reported one shows in **Reported**.

### Hide and delete

- **Hide** is reversible. Students see "This post was hidden by an instructor." in place of the post, with no author or text. **Unhide** brings it back as it was.
- **Delete** is final. Everyone, instructors included, sees "This post was deleted." and the text is gone.

Hiding or deleting a question hides its replies too. A hidden or deleted question leaves the Unanswered list.

## Limits

- Posts are plain text. Markup such as HTML is shown as typed, never rendered.
- A question title is up to 120 characters, and a question or reply up to 4,000 characters.
- A question holds up to 500 visible replies.

## Who can read them

Students with a current enrolment, on lessons they can open, and the course's instructors on any lesson. A student who dropped the course, a student not enrolled and visitors are refused. A student can't call the instructor's queues or moderation.

## Archived lessons

Archiving a lesson (removing it from a published course) hides it from students, so they can no longer open it or its questions. Instructors still read its threads under **By lesson**, and restoring the lesson brings the discussion back intact. A lesson with questions can stay archived but can't be deleted permanently.
