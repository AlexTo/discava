A quiz is marked automatically. An assignment is work a student uploads and an instructor grades.

## Quizzes: for instructors

In **Course content**, add a **Quiz** to a lesson. The quiz builder has:

- **Questions**: up to 50. Each is **Single choice** or **Multiple choice**, with 2 to 10 options and an optional **Explanation**.
- **Pass mark (%)**: from 0 to 100.
- **Attempts allowed**: **Unlimited**, or a number of at least 1.
- **Show the correct answers**: After each attempt, After the last attempt, or Never.
- **Shuffle the options for each student**.

There is no time limit.

**Preview as student** lets you take the quiz as a student would. Answers are checked and nothing is saved.

You can edit a quiz after students have attempted it. Their attempts and best scores are kept and never regraded. A student who is in the middle of an attempt is asked to check the changed questions before submitting.

## Quizzes: for students

The quiz shows its number of questions, the pass mark and how many attempts you have.

1. Choose **Start quiz**.
2. Answer each question. **Next** and **Back** move between them.
3. Choose **Review answers** to see what you answered and what is still unanswered. **Change** takes you back to a question.
4. Choose **Submit answers**.

The result shows this attempt's score and whether you passed. Your best score is the one that counts.

- **Resume quiz** carries on an attempt you left.
- **Retake quiz** starts another attempt, while you have attempts left.
- Passing the quiz, or using your last attempt, marks it complete.
- In an archived course, quizzes are closed.

## Assignments: for instructors

In a module, choose **New assignment** and pick its kind:

- **Upload**: students submit a file and you grade it.
- **Quiz**: a quiz that is graded automatically.

An upload assignment has an **Assignment title**, **Instructions**, **Max points** (a whole number from 1 to 1000) and an optional due date.

### Grading

**Home** lists the submissions **Waiting for grading**. An assignment's **Submissions** link in **Course content** lists every student, as Not submitted, Waiting, Resubmission requested or Returned.

1. Open a submission.
2. Read the file.
3. Enter the **Points**, from 0 to the maximum, and your **Feedback**.
4. Choose **Return** to give the student the grade, or **Request resubmission** to ask for another version.

After you return work you can still change the grade with **Save grade**. The student is told each time. You can read the work of a student who has left the course, but not grade it.

## Assignments: for students

The assignment shows its instructions, the **Maximum points** and the due date, if it has one.

1. Choose **Choose file** and pick your work.
2. Choose **Submit**.

- You submit one file of up to 100 MB: pdf, docx, xlsx, pptx, odt, ods, odp, txt, csv, md, png, jpg, jpeg, gif, webp or zip.
- **Late work is accepted.** After the due date your submission is marked **Late**.
- **Resubmit** replaces your file until the instructor returns it. Returned work can't be changed.
- If your instructor asks you to resubmit, you see their feedback and can submit a new version.
- Returned work shows your points and the feedback.
- In an archived course, work can no longer be submitted.

You are told in the bell, and by email unless you switch it off, when work is returned, when a resubmission is requested and when a grade changes. See <Link path="guides/notifications">Notifications</Link>.

Grades can count towards a certificate. See <Link path="guides/certificates">Certificates</Link>.
